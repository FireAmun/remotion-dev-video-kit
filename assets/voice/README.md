# Your voice reference goes here

Put an 8-15 second clip of **your own** voice here as `reference_energy.wav`, and its exact
transcript (what you say, word for word) as `reference_energy.txt`.

These files are git-ignored on purpose: never publish a clean sample of your voice,
anyone could clone it. See docs/GUIDE.pdf, chapter "Your voice", for how to record a good one.
